# Pilot checklist

The pilot of the platform at school, from the morning of **Wednesday 28 October 2026** (owner's answer 18, `docs/decisions.md`, Decided ahead for Phase 10), with several groups chosen by the school. It follows the go-live checklist (`checklist.md`). The owner approves this checklist; the Academic Vice Principal keeps its weekly table.

Rules of the pilot (`14`, Whole system, Pilot; `edge/academic-year`, the row "The pilot grade's teachers also teach other grades"):

- The **whole branch's** timetable is loaded, so clashes with lessons of other groups are real; only the pilot groups use journals.
- The **paper journal stays the official record** and runs alongside, until a written decision to switch.
- Before any switch, **one full quarter** of two or three real journals is entered and compared.

## 2. The order of data entry for the pilot

In this order, each step checked before the next (`edge/academic-year`, the setup order; `checklist.md`, section 4):

1. The branch, its rooms and departments.
2. The academic year, its four dated quarters, the go-live date (a school day).
3. Slot types, the default day template, the calendar.
4. Subjects and their weights for the quarters the timetable touches.
5. Staff with subjects, grades and availability; accounts with their roles (Teachers, Tutors, Head Teachers, the Academic Vice Principal of the branch).
6. Classes with their Tutors, the students of every class of the branch, and groups (the setup order of `edge/academic-year`; the whole branch is entered, only the pilot groups keep journals).
7. The teaching load of the whole branch.
8. The timetable built on the board, checked, and published from the go-live date.
9. The plan window opened (before the go-live date), so teachers can write their plans.
10. Each pilot teacher signs in once, changes the first password and finds their lessons on the home page.

Passwords: a forgotten password is reset by an administrator in the app (email is off, answer 16). A first password is valid for 7 days.

## 3. Every school day

- Teachers of the pilot groups mark attendance and type scores in the platform **and** in the paper journal.
- A problem, a wrong figure or a question goes through the **feedback button** on the page where it happened (it attaches the page and the role).

## 4. Every Friday: the comparison

The Academic Vice Principal compares **ten students' figures** in the platform with the same figures worked out from the paper journal (owner's answer 18).

- Choose ten students across the pilot groups, a different mix each week (some with absences, some with low scores, one transferred if any).
- For each: the attendance of the week, and the daily result of each pilot subject as the platform shows it.
- **The same** means the figure as shown on screen, after the one rounding the platform makes (`CLAUDE.md`: exact arithmetic, rounded once at the end). Work the paper figure by the same rule (`07`), not by a habit of the old journal.
- A difference is a **bug report**: send it through the feedback button from the student's journal page, naming the student's UID (not the name), the subject and the two figures.

## 5. At the end of the start quarter

- The Academic Vice Principal chooses for the branch: keep the platform results of the start quarter, or mark it "not official" (`checklist.md`, section 4).
- The quarter's results of the pilot journals are compared in full with the paper journal.

## 6. Before a switch

- [ ] One full quarter of two or three real journals entered and compared, with no open difference.
- [ ] A **written decision** by the school to switch, naming the groups and the date. Until then the paper journal stays the official record.
