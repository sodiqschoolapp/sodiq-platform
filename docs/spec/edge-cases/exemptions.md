# Edge cases: exemptions

> Part of the Sodiq School platform spec, baseline of 3 October 2026 (document revision 88).
> Index and reading order: `docs/spec/00-index.md`. Where an edge-case file is more specific than a module file, the edge-case file applies.

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A student with a year-long physical education exemption falls ill for three days. | Allowed. Two exemptions conflict only when their dates overlap and both cover all subjects, or both name the same subject. A cell is exempt if any exemption in force covers it; it shows 5 if one of them is full, otherwise 4. Ending one leaves the cell closed while another still covers it. |
| Full exemption for chosen slots of one day. | Not offered. A full exemption is always whole days. An exemption for chosen slots is temporary and covers every subject in that time. |
| Slots 5–7 on Friday are exempted, then Friday becomes a shortened day and a lesson moves into slot 6. | A slot exemption is stored as a time range on that date. Any held lesson of the student that overlaps it is exempt, including moved and substituted lessons. A lesson moved out of the range reopens. |
| An exemption was entered for the wrong student, or the student came back ten days early and nobody changed it. | Before the start date every field can be changed or the exemption cancelled. After it starts, only the end date changes; anything else is "void and enter a new one". There is no delete: "End" from a date, or "Void, entered in error" with a reason. Shortening into the past or voiding shows the same preview as a late entry. Cells get back the mark they had before; cells that had none open as optional. |
| A certificate for 1–10 October arrives on 20 October. On 3 October the student was marked present with a classwork score, and the lessons are locked. | Absent, skipped and empty cells in the range become exempt automatically. Each lesson with a "present" or "late" mark, or any number, needs a choice: keep as attended, or exempt and clear. The change is made by the Academic Vice Principal through the lock, logged under his name, and never counted as the teacher's late entry. |
| The range starts in a closed quarter and ends in an open one. | It is applied to the open part only. The closed part is applied after the affected journals of that quarter are reopened. |
| A surgery exemption runs from May to September. | No exemption extends past the last day of the academic year. The year rollover lists exemptions still in force as "to renew", and the Academic Vice Principal grants each again, reusing the document if wanted. |
| Nine students from five classes go to an olympiad. In flu week forty certificates arrive. | One form creates one exemption per chosen student, as a batch that can be ended or voided together. When a whole class is away, a bulk cancel of its lessons is used instead. |
| A Tutor recorded "illness, certificate" for three absences, but there is no exemption. | The Exemptions page shows the Academic Vice Principal a list of absences with a Tutor's reason and no exemption, with one click to create one. It is a list for him, not a request. |
| The reason is a diagnosis and the document is a medical certificate. | The reason is a category (illness, competition, family, school event, other), a detail text and a document. Teachers see kind, dates and scope. The class Tutor also sees the category. The detail and the document are for the Academic Vice Principal, Principal and Super Admin; each opening is logged, and both are left out of exports. |
| An exemption is created at 11:40 for today's remaining lessons, or changes last week's marks. | The Tutor and the teachers of the affected lessons are told the kind, dates and slots, without the reason. Same-day ones are sent at once and the cells change live. The Tutor's home page shows "exempt today". |
| A student with an exemption transfers, leaves, or moves to another branch. | The exemption belongs to the student and follows a transfer. It ends on leaving. On a branch move it goes to the new branch's Academic Vice Principal to confirm or drop. |
| The Academic Vice Principal is away for three weeks. | The Super Admin enters exemptions himself, as he can do everything the Academic Vice Principal does. There is no separate "acting" role; see People, roles and accounts. |
| Someone tries to type 4 or 5 in a cell. | Nobody can, including in a correction. These marks come only from exemptions. |
