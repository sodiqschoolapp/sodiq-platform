# Edge cases: calculation

> Part of the Sodiq School platform spec, baseline of 3 October 2026 (document revision 88).
> Index and reading order: `docs/spec/00-index.md`. Where an edge-case file is more specific than a module file, the edge-case file applies.

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A result lands exactly on a boundary. Weights 10 · 50 · 40, attendance 100, classworks 10, 10 and 25 out of 30, homework 29 out of 80: the exact result is 49.5, grade 3. Ordinary computer arithmetic can give 49.4999 and grade 2. | All arithmetic is done on exact fractions, from the number and total typed to the final result. Rounding happens once, at the end. Tests include totals of 3, 7 and 30 that land exactly on 49.5, 66.5 and 83.5. |
| Which value gives the grade? | The result rounded to a whole number. Thresholds such as "below 50" use the same rounded number. Ranking uses the exact value. |
| A part has a weight but no data: no homework was given all quarter. | The result is the weighted sum over the parts that have data for that student, divided by the sum of their weights, in open and closed quarters alike. The result carries a "part left out" marker, and the closing record stores which parts were used. The checklist warns when a part has a weight but no data in a whole journal. |
| Nothing at all was entered for a student. | The result is "No result": blank, not 0 and not a grade. |
| A student attends one lesson of forty, with no graded work, and gets 100. Another is absent all quarter and gets 0, grade 2, without ever being assessed. | In a graded subject, a result that rests on attendance alone is flagged. It blocks closing until the Academic Vice Principal chooses "keep the number" or "Not assessed". |
| A student joined late, returned, or started the subject mid-quarter and has few lessons. | The "fewer than one third" flag applies whatever the cause, not only exemptions. Count N = lessons held in the subject, in the journals the student belonged to, during the quarter. Count n = those where the student was expected. The flag is raised when n × 3 is less than N. It becomes a decision only on the closing checklist. The outcome is "keep", "Exempt" (when an exemption explains it) or "Not assessed". Decisions can be made for a whole journal at once. |
| The lesson score (AVG) of a lesson where one student has no classwork number, or has numbers for only some of the lesson's classworks. | The classwork part is the mean of the percents of the lesson's classworks that have a number for that student, and the homework part likewise for the homeworks checked at that lesson. A part with no number is left out; the score is calculated from the parts the student has, scaled to 100, and is blank if there is none. The lesson score is for display only and never enters a quarter figure. |
| A lesson has three classworks: 100%, 50% and 60%. The only other classwork of the quarter is 90%. | The lesson's classwork part is 70. The quarter classwork average counts each work once: (100 + 50 + 60 + 90) ÷ 4 = 75, so a lesson weighs as many times as it has works. |
| The month switch in the journal. | It shows the averages over that month's lessons. The month result uses the quarter's weights without the rating exam and is labelled so. |
| The rating exam. | One column per rating journal and quarter, with an exam date set by the teacher or the Academic Vice Principal. Whole numbers from 0 to 100. The journal's teacher enters scores from that date, with the same edit period and unlock as a lesson. Each student has a score, "did not sit" (counts 0, set by the teacher) or "exempt" (set only by the Academic Vice Principal). A late sitting or a retake replaces the score with a required reason; history is kept. |
| A student is exempt on the exam day. | The cell shows "exempt on exam day, pending". The teacher may later enter a score; otherwise the Academic Vice Principal sets "exempt". A pending entry blocks closing. |
| The exam is not held for one group this quarter. | The Academic Vice Principal marks the exam "not held" for that journal and quarter. The part is left out for everyone in it. |
| A student left the school before the quarter ended. | A "result at leaving" is stored on the profile: partial, labelled, with the number of lessons. The student is left out of the closing checklist, class averages, rating and the stipend list. |
| A student moved from one group to another in week 6. | The student's own figures use all their lessons and works in the subject during the quarter, across both journals. Class, group and teacher averages are the mean of the results of students who are members on the reference date; each student counts once. A moved-out student is shown folded with the full result and is outside that journal's averages. Attendance counts and lesson status stay with the journal where they were entered. |
| A student moved to a class that does not study the subject. | The subject gets a result from the lessons held, labelled "left the subject on \[date\]". The Academic Vice Principal decides at closing: keep, or "Not assessed". |
| A student moved between branches mid-quarter. | Results are not merged across branches. The old branch keeps a "result at leaving". The new branch calculates from its own lessons, and the few-lessons flag applies. |
| A student left and came back in the same quarter. | Lessons before leaving count. The gap shows closed cells, not absences. |
| Two students both show 84. | Order is by the exact value. Students share a place only when exactly equal, and places are numbered 1, 1, 3. Rating lists show one decimal. |
| One student has 9 subjects, another 11. | The all-subject figure is the plain mean of the graded subjects that have a number, with the count of subjects shown. |
| The year result when quarters show 50, 50, 49, 50 but are exactly 49.5, 49.5, 49.4, 49.5. | The year result is the mean of the quarter results that are numbers, each as published (rounded to a whole number as it is shown), rounded once more for the year: (50 + 50 + 49 + 50) ÷ 4 = 49.75, shown as 50, grade 3, so a report card adds up. The mean of the published whole numbers is kept exact until that last rounding. It is labelled "based on n of 4 quarters" when fewer than four have a number. The year grade is the band of the rounded year result. |
| Ma'naviyat soati and the Homework slot. | Attendance-only subjects have no weights, no lesson score, no result and no grade. They show an attendance average, are left out of the results table, rating and year result, and are included in attendance reports. |
| Quarterly weights are changed in week 7. | The change applies to open quarters only. Before saving, the system shows how many grades will change in each journal, asks for a reason and tells the subject's teachers. The journal legend shows "changed on \[date\]". |
| The value of "late" is changed from 50 to 75. | The values of "late" and "skipped" are stored per branch and quarter. They can change only while the quarter is open, with the same preview. A closed quarter keeps the values it was closed with, also when reopened. Grade bands are fixed for the whole school and are not a setting. |
| "Rating exam" is switched off, or a subject becomes attendance only, in week 5. | These switches are stored per quarter. They are free before the quarter's first lesson. In a started quarter the change is refused while scores exist. |
| The early-warning list compares a mid-quarter figure without the exam against last quarter's final with the exam. | It compares like with like: the "current work" figure (attendance, classwork, homework, no exam) against the same figure of the previous quarter and the same figure as it stood 28 days ago. "Five graded works" counts the student's own works. |
| A bug is found after results were closed and exported. | The calculation module has a version, stored with every final result. A "recalculate and compare" tool lists the differences without changing anything. Applying them is a separate action with a reason; the old results are kept and the teachers concerned are told. A release never changes closed results silently. |
