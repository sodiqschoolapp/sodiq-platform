# Edge cases: people, roles and accounts

> Part of the Sodiq School platform spec, baseline of 3 October 2026 (document revision 88).
> Index and reading order: `docs/spec/00-index.md`. Where an edge-case file is more specific than a module file, the edge-case file applies.

## Who can act

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| The Academic Vice Principal is ill for three weeks at the end of a quarter, or the post is vacant. Nobody can close the quarter, grant exemptions or open the plan window. | The Super Admin performs every one of these actions himself, and a branch with no active Academic Vice Principal shows an alert to the Super Admin. If one person should stand in for the whole period, the Super Admin gives the Academic Vice Principal role to that person's own account and removes it afterwards. This is ordinary role assignment: no separate "acting" role is built, and history shows who really did each action. |
| "Every account is created by an administrator": who is that? | The Super Admin creates staff accounts and gives all roles. The Academic Vice Principal creates student and relative records, edits a teacher's availability, subjects and grades, and marks teacher absences. He may reset passwords and lock accounts only for Teachers, Tutors, Head Teachers and the Head Tutor of his branch. The Academic Vice Principal also creates, edits, fills and closes vacancies, which are not accounts and hold no personal data. |
| One person is Teacher, Tutor and Head Teacher. | Rights are assignments: person, role, branch, and where needed subjects or a class, with dates. A Senior Head Teacher's assignment has subjects and no branch. The server allows an action if any current assignment permits it; the role switcher only changes what is displayed. The audit log records which assignment allowed the action. |
| The staff record allows one department, but a mathematics teacher is also a Tutor with Ma'naviyat soati. | A staff member has one teaching department, which governs their subjects. Tutor subjects come with the Tutor assignment of a class and are outside that rule. The category shown on the profile is the person's current roles in the branch, of every role, taken from the assignments, not typed (`04`, Teacher and staff record). |
| A person works in two branches. | One person, one account. A branch switcher sits beside the role switcher, and every request is checked against one branch. A teacher's home page shows the lessons of all their branches together, so teaching needs no switching. |
| A teacher moves to another department, or a subject is moved, mid-year. | Both changes carry a start date and are made by the Super Admin, and take effect on that date: until then the record shows the old department. They are refused while a teaching load would have a teacher outside the subject's department; those loads are listed for reassignment first. Open requests move to the new Head Teacher on the start date. |
| A request waits for a Head Teacher who has been replaced. | Tasks that need action are addressed to the post, not the person, and shown to whoever holds it now. With no holder they go to the Academic Vice Principal, then to the Super Admin. |
| The Principal is "view only": can he export or add a note? | The server refuses every change by the Principal except those to his own account: password, language, notification settings and photo, his sessions and sign-out, his second step and his trusted devices, sending a feedback report about the app (Quality plan, Monitoring), and reading his own notifications and marking them as read. Exports are allowed, without contact and identity fields, and are logged. |

## Staff over time

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A teacher is dismissed today. The system refuses "left" because future lessons depend on them, so the account stays open. | Locking an account is separate from employment status. "Lock now" ends all sessions at once, needs no reassignment and is logged with a reason. The teacher's future lessons go to "needs a teacher". |
| A teacher goes on four months' leave; another gives notice for the 30th. | Active: can be scheduled and can sign in. Inactive: temporarily not working, cannot be scheduled or sign in, roles kept. Left: all roles ended. Each status change has a date, which can be in the future; lessons from that date are listed for reassignment. |
| A teacher leaves with open requests; a Head Teacher leaves with requests waiting for them. | On the date, their own requests are cancelled and their unlocks end. Requests waiting for their approval stay with the post: the new Head Teacher sees them, and while the post is empty they go to the Academic Vice Principal. |
| A teacher who left is hired again, or is entered twice with different spellings. | The staff record has a JSHSHIR field, unique when filled. The system also warns on the same name and date of birth. A phone that belongs to another adult is refused and that record is shown, since one phone number belongs to one adult (close relatives edge cases). Re-hiring restores the same record, staff ID and login with a new employment period and a forced password change. |
| Two branches save a new teacher at the same moment; a teacher who left in 2026 is hired again in 2028. | The staff ID is S-YYNNNN: "S", a hyphen, the last two digits of the year of the person's first admission date, and the next four-digit number of that year, taken when the record is first saved (S-260001 is the first person hired in 2026). The admission date is required to save a staff record. There is one counter per year for the whole school, so the ID is the same in every branch and two records never get the same number. The ID belongs to the person for good: a re-hire keeps it, and a later correction of the admission date does not change it. It is never reused, including the IDs of archived records. A 10,000th hire in one year is refused. |

## Accounts and sign-in

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| How does the first account come to exist? What if the only Super Admin leaves? | The first Super Admin is created by a one-time command on the server, which is logged. The system refuses to lock or remove the last active Super Admin, and nobody can remove their own Super Admin role. A banner shows while fewer than two exist. |
| An administrator resets a teacher's password, signs in as that teacher and changes marks. | A reset gives a one-time password valid for 24 hours and forces a change before anything else. It ends all sessions, is logged as "reset by X for Y", and the user sees "your password was reset by X" at next sign-in. A report lists entries made within 48 hours of a reset. There is no "sign in as another user" function. A password reset by email that follows a change of the email by somebody else is listed in the same report. |
| Two-step sign-in is required, but SMS comes only in Release 2. The phone is lost on the day the quarter must be closed. | The second step is a code from an authenticator app, with ten single-use recovery codes shown at set-up. A Super Admin resets an Academic Vice Principal's second step; another Super Admin resets a Super Admin's. |
| The site is reachable during the pilot with real data and no limit on sign-in attempts. | The attempt limit exists before any real personal data is loaded. An unknown login and a wrong password get the same answer. After five failures each further attempt is delayed; accounts are never locked permanently by failures. Limits are per account and device, not per network address, because the whole school shares one. Passwords have at least ten characters. Logins are generated (for example a.karimov) and never reused. |
| A role is removed while the person has the journal open. | Rights are read again on every request. A refused save shows "you no longer have access to this journal" and keeps the typed values visible. |
| One account is open on a classroom computer, a phone and at home. | Several sessions are allowed. The profile lists them, with "sign out everywhere". A password change ends all others. Role and branch on display are kept per tab. **Trust this device.** The sign-in page has a "Trust this device" switch, off by default, with the note "do not use on a shared computer". When it is on, that browser stays signed in for 7 days from the sign-in, with no idle sign-out. The sign-in that starts the trust still asks for the second step where the account has one. After 7 days the person signs in again, second step included. Trust ends at once on "sign out", "sign out everywhere", a password change or reset, and an account lock. The profile lists trusted devices and each can be removed; removing one ends its session at once. A sign-in with trust is recorded in the sign-in history. Within the 7 days a trusted device is never asked for the password again, whatever the action. This also holds for the Super Admin and Academic Vice Principal. The one exception is changing one's own password, which always needs the current one. Every session ends 7 days after sign-in at the latest, trusted or not. |
| A teacher changes their own email and then resets the password by email. | Staff can change their own password (with the current one), language, notification settings and photo. Phone, email and name are changed by the Super Admin or Academic Vice Principal; the Academic Vice Principal changes an email only for the accounts whose passwords he may reset, and nobody changes the email of their own record. |
| 400 first passwords must be handed out. Later, when a staff import is added, a file contains a "role" column with Super Admin. | Only the Super Admin imports staff, and an import can create only Teachers and Tutors. First passwords are random, valid for 7 days, printed once on per-person slips and not stored. A report lists accounts never used. |

## Personal data and the audit log

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A Tutor, a Head Teacher or the Principal opens a student profile and sees document numbers, address, relatives' workplaces and the scholarship. | Fields are grouped and each group has named readers; each role sees the data related to its work. Basic (name, class, photo, UID): all staff of the branch, except that the Teacher role reads only the students the teacher teaches (the members of their classes and groups in the teaching load in force) and no other student; a Teacher with no classes reads no student. Contact (phones of student and relatives): Academic Vice Principal, Principal, Head Tutor, Vice Principal for Spiritual and Moral Education, and the class Tutor. Identity (document numbers, address, date of birth, relatives' workplaces and emails): Super Admin, Academic Vice Principal and Head Tutor; the Principal sees them hidden, with a logged "show". Financial (contract, scholarship, stipend): Super Admin, Academic Vice Principal, Principal, Head Tutor. The diagnostic test score: the readers of Financial, the Head Teachers, and the Tutor of the student's class. The Head Tutor reads every group of every student of the branch. |
| A Tutor ticks every student of the branch and exports. | An export contains only what the role may see. Exports with contact or identity fields, of the whole branch or of ticked rows, are for the Super Admin, the Academic Vice Principal and the Head Tutor, who exports every field group of the branch's students. Head Teachers export the Basic fields. A Tutor exports the students of own class with every field the Tutor reads about them, and no other class; teachers export their own journals. Every export is logged with its filters and row count. |
| Who may read the audit log, and what is in it? | The Super Admin reads all; the Principal and Academic Vice Principal read their branch, including the changes of a relative made while the relative is linked to a student of the branch; sign-ins and school-wide settings are read by the Super Admin; every user sees their own sign-in history. It records sign-ins, password and two-step events, role and status changes, create, edit, archive and restore, forced delete, exports, showing hidden fields, approvals, unlocks, closing and reopening, imports. Ordinary page views are not recorded. It can be searched by person, record and date. |
| Search with Ctrl+K finds anyone in any branch. | Search returns only records the person may open, in the current branch. Search by phone or document number is for the Super Admin and Academic Vice Principal. Relatives' phones are searched too, for the Super Admin, the Principal and the Academic Vice Principal, and a relative found by phone lists the relative's children. |
| A Super Admin force-deletes a lesson with marks. | Refused in a closed quarter. It notifies the branch's Academic Vice Principal, the Principal and the teacher. Only a Super Admin restores it, within 30 days; after that the data is removed and a summary stays in the audit log. |
| The developer has access to the live database. | There is no permanent developer account in the live system. When someone must look inside the live system, a Super Admin gives that person an ordinary account with a role, audited like any other. No live data is copied to development or test. Error reports carry no names, phones or marks. |
| A parent asks for a copy of everything held about their child, or for deletion after leaving. | The Super Admin can produce a personal data report per person. Deletion is not offered: the school keeps data with no time limit and the platform erases nothing. The consent text signed at enrolment should say so. Consent is recorded on each student with its date. |
| Later, on the permission matrix screen, a Super Admin gives the Principal an edit right. | Some rules cannot be changed on that screen: the Principal has no edit rights, exemptions belong to the Academic Vice Principal and the Super Admin, and branches, years and forced delete belong to the Super Admin. Each change needs a reason and shows who gains and loses what. The screen is built after go-live. |
