# Edge cases: close relatives (Parents)

> Part of the Sodiq School platform spec, baseline of 3 October 2026 (document revision 88).
> Index and reading order: `docs/spec/00-index.md`. Where an edge-case file is more specific than a module file, the edge-case file applies.

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A father has a daughter in one branch and a son in another. | Relatives are school-wide records, and the phone is unique across the whole school. Staff can open a relative only if a student of their own branch is linked; students of other branches appear as a count. A change of name or phone is logged and the other branch's Academic Vice Principal is told. |
| A new student's mother gives a number that is stored as the father of a student who left years ago: the operator gave the number to someone else. | On a phone match the person entering chooses "same person" (link) or "different person, the number has a new owner". The second choice asks for the earlier relative's new phone. If it is unknown, that relative is set to "no valid phone" and listed for the Academic Vice Principal while a linked student is active. Sign-in does not use the phone, so the Parent account is not affected. |
| The same mother was entered twice with two phones. | When a phone change is refused because the number exists, "Merge" is offered while the holder is linked to a student, and "different person, the number has a new owner" always. The Academic Vice Principal picks the name and phone that stay; all links move to one record. Across branches, only the Super Admin merges. |
| Mother and father share one phone. | The second parent is added as a relative "with no phone of their own". The uniqueness rule applies to relatives who have a phone. Sign-in and the digest do not use the phone: the second parent gets a login, a slip and the digest like any other. If they also share one Telegram account, that chat links to one of the two accounts; the other uses the portal in a browser. |
| A father works abroad and has a foreign number; a landline is given. | Phones are stored in international format with +998 as the default; other country codes are accepted. Extra phones can be added; they are not unique. |
| The main contact is unlinked from a student. | Each student has exactly one main contact. Unlinking it requires choosing another in the same action. A student's only relative is the main contact automatically. |
| Divorced parents; an uncle listed for emergencies. In Release 2 all of them would see the child's marks. | Every relative linked to a student has a Parent account, whatever the relationship. Each link between a relative and a student carries: relationship, main contact, portal access and "receives notices". Portal access is on by default for every link and can be switched off for one link, for example by a court decision (proposed). Only links with portal access show the child's data, in the portal and in the parents' digest; the digest also needs "receives notices" on the link. Both are on by default. A change to either switch applies from the next request and to every digest not yet sent. The fields exist from Release 1. |
| A teacher is also a parent, and the staff phone is the relative's phone. | The staff record and the relative record stay separate and are marked as the same person. The system sets the mark when a relative's main phone equals a staff member's phone, in both directions (a new relative, a new staff member, a changed phone), and the person's name and details are taken from the existing record. Nobody sets or removes the mark by hand; correcting a phone updates it, and the audit log records the change. The mark compares current phone numbers only: when a relative's phone equals a staff member's current phone and they are not the same person, the staff member's phone is corrected first, which removes the match, and the relative is then saved as a new person. One phone number belongs to one adult, a staff member or a relative; a student's own phone may repeat a relative's. In Release 2 the Parent account has its own login and password, apart from the staff account: a parent session never carries staff rights, and the staff account does not show the parent view. Marks a staff member enters for their own child are listed in a report for the Academic Vice Principal and Principal. |
| A young student's "own phone" is the mother's number. | A student's own phone may repeat a relative's: it is contact data only. Students have no sign-in in the first version of Release 2; how they sign in is decided when the student portal is planned. |
| First passwords for a class of 25 students with 40 relatives; a mother has children in two classes and two branches; a father already signed in. | The Academic Vice Principal (own branch) and the Super Admin print the slips of a class, four to an A4 page; the student takes the slip home, or it is handed out at a parents' meeting. A first password cannot be sent through Telegram: the bot can reach a parent only after the parent has signed in. A slip is printed for each relative linked to a student of the class with portal access on who has no password of their own yet: printing gives a new first password, random, valid for 30 days, printed once and not stored. A relative whose slip was printed in another class and is still valid is listed with that class and date and not printed again, unless the person printing chooses "print again", which replaces the earlier password. A relative who already chose a password is listed as "signed in". One account per relative, whatever the number of children. Every printing is in the audit log; a report lists Parent accounts never used. |
| A parent forgets the password; the phone with the linked Telegram chat is lost or passed on. | The Academic Vice Principal of a branch where one of the parent's children studies, or the Super Admin, resets it: a new one-time password on a slip, valid for 7 days, changed at first sign-in; every session and the Telegram link end, and it is logged as "reset by X for Y". "Unlink Telegram" on the Parent page ends the link at once without a reset. The parent changes their own password, language and digest frequency in the portal; name, phone and email stay with staff. |
