# Roles and access

> Part of the Sodiq School platform spec, baseline of 3 October 2026 (document revision 88).
> Index and reading order: `docs/spec/00-index.md`. Where an edge-case file is more specific than a module file, the edge-case file applies.

There are 16 user types. One person has one account and can hold several roles (for example Teacher, Tutor and Head Teacher); a role switcher in the top bar changes the menu and home page.

| Role | Sees | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Super Admin | All branches | Everything every other role can do, including entering exemptions, closing quarters and every other action of the Academic Vice Principal. Only this role creates branches, buildings, floors and rooms, divisions, roles and permissions, academic years, and awards the quarterly stipend. Only this role can force-delete. At most 3 accounts. |
| Principal | Own branch, everything | Views everything in the branch: timetables, all journals, lists, analytics. Changes nothing. |
| Academic Vice Principal | Own branch | Full edit of students, classes, groups, subjects and their weights, academic calendar, slot types and day templates, timetable. Grants exemptions. Opens the lesson plan window and can approve any plan edit request. Sees the teacher list and all journals; can correct a journal with a recorded reason. Closes quarters. Analytics. Creates, edits, fills and closes vacancies (People, "Vacancy"); they are planning records, not accounts. |
| Vice Principal for Spiritual and Moral Education | Own branch | Views attendance for all classes and attendance analytics. Sees the student list of the branch (view only; the last column of the list shows the attendance percent only, not the average). Opens a student's profile, view only: Overview with the Basic and Contact fields, and the Attendance, Timetable and Relatives tabs (name, relationship and phones of a relative). Events in Release 2. |
| Head Tutor | Own branch | Views all classes, their Tutors and Class teachers, and attendance by class and by Tutor or Class teacher. Sees the school timetable and the timetable of every class of the branch. Sees the student list of the branch (view only) and reads every field group of every student of the branch, the financial group included; exports the student list. Opens a student's profile, view only: Overview with every field group, and the Attendance, Timetable, Relatives and Contract and scholarship tabs. |
| Tutor | Own class or classes; student list of the branch | Views the full student list of the branch (view only) and the profiles of own class. Exports the students of own class with every field the Tutor reads about them; no other class. Sees the school timetable and the timetable of own class, attendance per lesson and daily attendance in percent. Holds the Ma'naviyat soati and Homework slot lessons of own class: they are always held by the class's Tutor in force on the lesson's date (the acting Tutor while one is set), and nobody picks their teacher by hand. Records the reason for an absence, without changing the mark. |
| Class teacher (Sinf rahbari) | Own class (one class of grades 0–4); student list of the branch | Takes the Tutor's place in a class of grades 0–4: everything the Tutor sees and does for own class, the Ma'naviyat soati and Homework slot lessons included. Has one own class at a time; an acting Class teacher of another class has that class too for the period. Also works as a Teacher: teaches subjects of own class (and of other classes) through the teaching load like any teacher, and keeps their journals; other teachers may teach subjects of the class too. Overseen by the Head Tutor. |
| Senior Head Teacher | Own subjects in all branches | Everything a Head Teacher can do, for the subjects given to them, in every branch. Sees the Head Teachers of those subjects and the requests waiting for them. Can keep a master plan for all branches. Also works as a Teacher. |
| Head Teacher | Own subjects in own branch | Oversees the subjects given to them in a branch. Full student list across classes and groups; exports it with the Basic fields. Views journals and lesson plans of those subjects, and the timetables of the teachers who teach them. Moves students between groups of those subjects. Approves unlock requests and requests to edit a lesson plan after the window, for those subjects. Keeps master plans. Also works as a Teacher. |
| Teacher | Own lessons; the students they teach | Own timetable. The students they teach (the members of their classes and groups in the teaching load in force), with the Basic fields only, and no other student. Journals of own classes and groups: attendance, marks, comments. Rating. Lesson plans. |
| Student | Self | Own timetable, attendance, marks, homework. Release 2, after the parent portal. |
| Parent | Own children | For each child whose link has portal access on: timetable, homework, grades with the comments visible to parents, attendance, in the portal (a Telegram mini app), plus a digest in the Telegram bot. Changes no data. Release 2. |
| HR, Nurse, Psychologist, Librarian | Own branch | Own module. Release 3. |

## Head Teachers and Senior Head Teachers

- **Subjects, not departments.** A Head Teacher oversees a list of subjects in one branch. The Super Admin makes the appointment and chooses the subjects: for example one Head Teacher for IELTS, General English and SAT, and another for Mathematics and AP Calculus. The subjects may come from different departments.
- **One per subject per branch.** A subject in a branch has at most one Head Teacher, so every request has one clear approver. A subject can have none.
- **Scope.** For own subjects in own branch: journals, lesson plans, groups, the early-warning list, and the timetables of the teachers who teach them. A teacher of Mathematics and Physics is seen by the Mathematics Head Teacher only in Mathematics.
- **Senior Head Teacher.** Appointed by the Super Admin with a list of subjects, which can be all subjects. A subject has at most one Senior Head Teacher at a time. A Senior Head Teacher sees and does everything a Head Teacher can for those subjects, in every branch, and chooses the branch with a filter.
- **Who approves.** A teacher's unlock or plan request goes to the Head Teacher of the subject in that branch. The Senior Head Teacher of the subject and the Academic Vice Principal of the branch see it too, and either can decide it. With no Head Teacher it goes to those two.
- **Own requests.** Nobody approves their own. A Head Teacher's own go to the Senior Head Teacher of the subject or to the Academic Vice Principal. A Senior Head Teacher's own go to the Academic Vice Principal of the branch where the lesson is. An Academic Vice Principal's own go to the Super Admin.
- **Departments stay.** A department groups subjects and teachers, and a teacher's subjects come from one department, except subjects taught only in grades 0–4, which may come from any department (a Class teacher teaches mathematics, native language and reading to one class). It has no head, except Tutors, whose head is the Head Tutor; the Head Tutor also oversees the Class teachers.
- **Changes.** Appointments carry dates. When a subject passes to another Head Teacher, its open requests pass with it.

## Rules

- Every role is limited to a branch, and where it applies to subjects, a class or own lessons. Only the Super Admin and the Senior Head Teacher work across branches. The limit is enforced on the server, not only hidden in the menu.
- Permissions are a table: rows are roles, columns are actions (view, create, edit, archive, export) per area. At first the table is fixed in code for the 16 roles. A screen where the Super Admin edits it and adds new roles comes later; it changes where the table is kept, not how the checks work.
- Parents see only their own children. Medical and psychologist records are visible only to the roles that own them.
- Every sign-in, change, archive and export is written to an audit log that nobody can edit.
- There is no open sign-up. Every account is created by an administrator. Staff sign in with login and password and must change the password at first sign-in. Two-step verification is required for the Super Admin and Academic Vice Principal and optional for others. From Release 2 parents also sign in with a login and password, given on a printed slip and changed at first sign-in, without two-step verification; an administrator resets a forgotten password. Students have no sign-in in the first version of Release 2.
- Admissions manager and accountant, which appear in the older documents, are not created now.

## Integration keys

Some departments keep a platform of their own. An integration key lets such a platform do, through the API, the work of the people on the key, as if they typed it in this platform (owner, 21 November 2026).

- **What a key is.** The Super Admin creates a key with a name (for example "English platform"), a list of people and an end date at most one year ahead. Each person on the list holds the Teacher, Head Teacher or Senior Head Teacher role. One person can be on several keys. The secret is shown once, when the key is created, and is stored only as a hash. Only the Super Admin creates keys, changes their people and end date, and revokes them. The list of keys shows each key's last use.
- **Acting for a person.** Every request made with a key names one person on that key, by staff ID. The server allows it only while the key is not revoked and not past its end date, and the person is on the key and active. It checks this again on every request.
- **What a person may do through a key.** Exactly what their Teacher, Head Teacher and Senior Head Teacher assignments allow in this platform, and nothing more. The same branch, subjects, own classes and own lessons apply, and so do the same edit periods, locks, cell versions and checks. Their other roles do not count through a key. Never through a key: exports, a student's record (profile, diagnostic score, history; the names in a journal, class or group are not a record), moving students between groups, and the person's own account (password, second step, sessions, trusted devices, language, notification settings, photo). The routes open to keys are a list fixed in code; a route added later stays closed to keys until a task adds it.
- **Calculation stays here.** A key sends what a person types: attendance marks, works and their totals, the numbers typed, topics, comments, plans. Percents, averages, results and grades are calculated only by this platform. A key can read them but never sends them.
- **Audit.** Every change made through a key is recorded as the person's change, with the key named ("via English platform").
- **Not a sign-in.** A key does not sign the person in: it opens no session and touches nothing of their account. "There is no sign in as another user" still holds.
- **Limits and keeping.** The server limits how many requests a key may make per minute. The Super Admin hands the key to the platform's owner, who keeps it on that platform's server, never in a page or app that runs on a user's device.

## Assistant

The assistant lets a person work by writing in plain words, in Uzbek, Russian or English: "we had a power cut, cancel lessons 4 to 8 today", "make 8 and 9 March days off", "move 7B's mathematics from Tuesday to Thursday", "which classes have no Tutor". It uses an outside language model, Google Gemini, on a paid plan (owner, 21 November 2026).

- **Who has it.** The Super Admin, the Academic Vice Principal and the Teacher, in the role chosen in the role switcher. A person whose chosen role is another role does not see it. The Super Admin can switch it off for the whole school or for one of the three roles; it is off until switched on.
- **Where.** A button in the top bar opens a chat panel beside the page; on a phone it fills the screen. The panel answers in the person's language.
- **Acting as the person.** The assistant has no rights of its own. It works only through the platform's own actions, with the person's session and chosen role: the same branch, subjects, own classes and own lessons, and the same edit periods, locks, record versions and checks as the screens. The actions open to it are a list fixed in code for each of the three roles; an action added later stays closed to it until a task adds it.
- **Reading and proposing.** It reads at once. A change is never made by the assistant alone: it is shown as a proposal that lists every change in plain words, with the platform's own preview where the action has one (the impact preview of a calendar change, the lessons a bulk cancel touches). Nothing changes until the person presses Confirm. Each change then goes through the same action as on the screen, in order; the first change refused stops the rest, and the answer lists what was done and what was not. A proposal not confirmed within 15 minutes expires, and a change proposed on data that has changed since is refused as outdated.
- **Asking instead of guessing.** When a request is unclear or could mean two things ("cancel Friday": which classes, which slots?), the assistant asks. When no action can do what was asked, or the rules forbid it, it says so and names the way that exists ("today cannot become a day off; a bulk cancel from lesson 4 can").
- **What the Super Admin and the Academic Vice Principal can do through it.** Within what their role allows: rooms and buildings (Super Admin), the academic calendar, slot types and day templates, academic years and quarters (dates), subjects and their weights, staff records and availability, classes, groups, students and their memberships, the teaching load, the imports through their own preview, one-day changes, bulk cancels, absences, substitutions, exams, vacancies, the lesson plan window, and the switches of portal access. Reading: everything their role reads, except the fields below.
- **What a Teacher can do through it.** Read their own timetable, their classes and groups, their journals and the results calculated by the platform, their lesson plans and requests. Draft lesson plan items (topic, works, homework) and send unlock and plan edit requests, each as a proposal to confirm.
- **Never through the assistant.** Entering or changing journal data (attendance, numbers, totals, comments, "not held"), journal corrections, exemptions, closing a quarter; publishing or withdrawing a timetable version; starting or closing a year; force delete; accounts (passwords, second step, sessions, printed slips), roles, appointments and permissions; integration keys; exports. For these it opens the right page and leaves the step to the person.
- **What never goes to the model.** Passport and birth certificate numbers, medical data, the financial group, phone numbers and addresses, passwords and secrets. Names, classes, timetables, plans and results go only as far as the request needs them.
- **Calculation stays here.** The assistant reads percents, averages, results and grades from the platform and quotes them. It never calculates a result of its own.
- **Audit.** Every change made through the assistant is recorded as the person's change, marked "via assistant", and the audit log shows the request it came from. The conversation is kept: what was asked, what was proposed, and whether it was confirmed, declined or expired. The person sees their own conversations; the Super Admin sees all of them. 90 days after its last message a conversation is archived: it leaves the person's list, and the Super Admin reads it and can restore it. Conversations are never deleted.
- **Limits.** A limit of requests per person per day, and a monthly spending cap for the school: $300 at the start (owner, 21 November 2026), which the Super Admin can change. When either is reached, or the model cannot be reached, the panel says so; every screen works as before. Nothing else in the platform depends on the assistant.
- **Text inside the data is not an instruction.** Comments, feedback and names are written by many people. The assistant treats everything it reads as data, never as an instruction, and only the person's own words and Confirm can lead to a change.

## Accounts and sign-in: build order

Sign-in starts simple and grows. Permission checks do not: they are in place from the first piece of work, because every later screen depends on them.

| When | Sign-in | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| From the first piece of work | Accounts created by an administrator. Login and password. Password change at first sign-in. Password reset by an administrator. Sessions end after a period without use. | Every record carries its branch. Every request passes one permission check on the server. A short list of routes works without sign-in: the health check and sign-in (all its steps), later password reset by email, and from Release 2 the Telegram bot's webhook, which checks the secret Telegram sends with each update. Each is declared public in code, and a test holds the exact list. Tests for what each role can and cannot do. Roles and permissions fixed in code. |
| Before the school goes live | Two-step sign-in for the Super Admin and Academic Vice Principal. A limit on failed sign-in attempts. Password reset by email. | No change: roles and permissions stay fixed in code. The permission matrix screen is built after go-live. |
| Release 2 | Login and password for parents, on slips printed class by class; signing in once in the Telegram mini app links the parent's chat. No SMS and no sign-in codes. Students later. | Parent access switched on; student access with the student portal. |
| After go-live (owner, 21 November 2026) | Integration keys for the department platforms (Integration keys, above). No sign-in and no session. | The same permission check for the named person, limited to the routes open to keys. A test holds that list. |
| After go-live (owner, 21 November 2026) | The assistant (Assistant, above), inside the person's own session. | The same permission check for the person and the chosen role, limited to the actions open to the assistant for that role. A test holds each list. |
