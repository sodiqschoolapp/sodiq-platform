# Spec changes

The spec in `docs/spec/` was taken from the full spec document on 3 October 2026 (revision 88). Every later change to a spec file is listed here, newest first, with the date, what changed, why, and the files touched. Changes are made only with the `/spec-change` skill and the owner's approval.

## 7 November 2026: date of birth, scholarship and relatives of a student are optional

- What changed:
  1. **Date of birth is optional** on a student, by hand and in the import.
  2. **The scholarship is optional**: with none chosen, the type is None (the stored default since 0032).
  3. **Relatives are optional.** A student can be saved with no relative, by hand or by the import, and the last relative of a student can be removed ("Always connected" becomes "Relatives are optional"). When a student has relatives, exactly one is the main contact. A student with no relative has no parent access in the portal.
  4. **The duplicate warning** on a matching name and date of birth also warns on a matching name alone when either record has no date of birth.
- Why: the owner, 7 November 2026, in chat: the school does not always have these when a student is entered; the owner approved the wording and chose the warning on the name alone.
- Code: built at once in its own pull request (branch `fix/optional-student-fields`), not a build-plan task; its migration is numbered after 10.8's 0162 and merges after 10.8. Database: `students.birth_date` becomes nullable and the triggers of "at least one relative" (0036, `student_relatives_guard`) are dropped (**Look first**: a constraint removed because the requirement changed).
- Files: `docs/spec/04-people.md` (Student record: Personal, Close relatives, Payment; Excel import; Close relatives: "Relatives are optional"), `docs/spec/edge-cases/students-classes-groups.md` (the duplicate row), `docs/spec/edge-cases/close-relatives.md` (the main contact unlinked).

## 7 November 2026: the journal and the plan as one, files of each work, the kind of a work, the lesson happening now

- What changed:
  1. **A work added in the journal goes into the plan.** At a held lesson with no plan item, a classwork or "homework given" added in the journal (by the teacher or the substitute, as before) is written into the lesson's plan item, created for it with the plan when there is none, and marked in the plan's history as added in the journal; the plan's editor shows it. The topic stays empty until the owner writes it. Works added by hand before this change stay as they are, and the take-over by a plan item written later still applies to them.
  2. **The lesson card edits the plan item.** The plan's owner changes the topic, objectives and links, materials and works of a lesson on its lesson card, under the plan's own rules: freely while the row is open (window or approved request), otherwise the card offers the edit request. A substitute still cannot change the item. "The plan is read only in the journal" is gone.
  3. **Files of each work.** A classwork and a homework can carry their own files; the files of a homework are what students and parents see with it (Release 2). The item's "Materials" files are for staff only. Files can be attached while a row is being written. Limits: 20 MB per file, at most 10 in the materials and 10 on each work.
  4. **The kind of a work in the journal.** A work column's title has a small coloured tag with the letter of its kind (S/U in Uzbek, К/Д in Russian, C/H in English), the full word on hover.
  5. **The lesson happening now.** On a teacher's home page that lesson's row is highlighted and its "Open the journal" button pulses gently; no motion for people who ask for reduced motion. `docs/design-system.md`, Motion, names this one exception to "nothing moves on its own".
- Why: the owner, 7 November 2026, in chat: teachers could not tell classwork from homework once works had titles; a homework added in the journal was missing from the plan; the lesson card could add works but not the topic or objectives; parents saw every file of a lesson, including a teacher's answer key (issue #42, 11.2 Look first); a teacher who opens the platform in class should find the lesson's journal at once. The owner asked for this change and confirmed it without a wording round.
- Code: tasks 10.7 (items 4 and 5, and the journal's columns hidden by default except the photo, a choice still kept per browser) and 10.8 (items 1 to 3) of `docs/build-plan.md` (not built), added with this change. Built code that changes: the add route of works (`apps/api/src/modules/journal/works.service.ts`) and the take-over (`works-takeover.ts`), the lesson card (`lesson-card.tsx`, read only today), the plan files (`lesson_plan_files` per item, `plan-row-panel.tsx`), the portal's homework files (`portal-homework.service.ts`), the grid header (`journal-grid.tsx`), the teacher home (`teaching-home.tsx`). Existing plan files become materials (staff only) with no row changed, so families stop seeing them until a teacher attaches files to a homework; existing hand-added works are not moved into plans (that would change rows, stop-list item 3). No calculation changes.
- Files: `docs/spec/07-journal.md` (Layout, Lessons; Entering data, Classwork and homework, Topic), `docs/spec/09-lesson-plans.md` (What a plan contains: Classwork, Homework, Objectives and materials, the form; In the journal; When something else changes, a substitute), `docs/spec/edge-cases/journal.md` (the row "A lesson is held with no plan item"), `docs/spec/edge-cases/lesson-plans.md` (who may read a plan; the limits of files), `docs/spec/10-portal-notifications-release-2.md` (What parents see, Homework), `docs/spec/12-ux-standards.md` (Home page); also `docs/design-system.md` (Motion), which is not a spec file.

## 6 November 2026: the parent portal in Telegram, sign-in with login and password, the digest

- What changed:
  1. **Parents sign in with a login and password**, like staff, without two-step sign-in. The first password is random, valid for 30 days, printed once on a slip and changed at first sign-in. The Academic Vice Principal (own branch) and the Super Admin print the slips class by class, four to an A4 page; the student takes the slip home. A forgotten password is reset by the Academic Vice Principal of a branch where one of the parent's children studies, or by the Super Admin (a new slip, valid for 7 days). One account per relative. No SMS and no sign-in codes anywhere in the platform. The main phone stays unique (one phone, one adult) but is no longer used for sign-in, so "no valid phone" no longer blocks sign-in and a second parent who shares a phone gets a login too. A staff member who is also a parent has two accounts; a parent session never carries staff rights.
  2. **The portal is a Telegram mini app** inside the school's bot (the platform's own pages, also open in a browser), phone first and read only. The first version is for parents only and shows, for each child whose link has portal access on: timetable, homework, grades with work names and the comments visible to parents, attendance. Rating, announcements, the stipend history and the student portal come later in Release 2. Signing in once in the mini app links the Telegram chat to the account; while the link holds, opening the mini app from that chat needs no password (Telegram's signature is checked every time). One account to one chat. The link ends on sign-out, a password change or reset, and "unlink Telegram". The bot's webhook joins the routes that work without sign-in.
  3. **The digest.** Notices to parents are gathered in a digest from the bot: daily at 19:00, twice a week (Wednesday and Saturday) or weekly (Saturday), in Tashkent time; the parent chooses, twice a week until chosen; the Super Admin sets the time. Per child (portal access and "receives notices" both on, both on by default): an attendance summary line, then absences and late arrivals; grades with work names as they stand at send time; comments visible to parents; for what was saved since the previous digest. A later correction appears in the next digest marked "corrected" or "removed". Nothing new, no message. Each message is checked again just before it is sent. The parent's language, Uzbek until chosen. A queue with retries, quiet hours (21:00 to 08:00 by default), a send log. No instant absence message in the first version.
  4. **Staff notices** stay in the app; the SMS channel is removed from every row. Instant messages to families (absence, timetable changes, quarter results, announcements) come later in Release 2, through the bot.
- Why: the owner, 6 November 2026, in chat: the parent portal comes first in Release 2 and is urgent; parents sign in like staff; the Telegram bot is the main channel and the portal a mini app inside it; a digest, not one message per event. The owner accepted the recommended answers to the five questions (digest times; grades as they stand with corrections marked later; absence in the digest only; what the first version shows; who prints the slips) and approved the twelve readings proposed with the wording, with the attendance summary line added and slips given through the students (a first password cannot be sent through Telegram before the parent has signed in).
- Code: the Release 2 tasks 11.1 to 11.5 of `docs/build-plan.md` (not built), added with this change. Release 1 code: Parent accounts exist (`relative_accounts`, 0036) switched off and without a login; student accounts (`student_accounts`, 0032) stay switched off. No calculation and no constraint changes with this entry. The comments of migrations 0032 and 0036 that mention SMS sign-in are old text in merged migrations and stay.
- Files: `docs/spec/10-portal-notifications-release-2.md` (rewritten: What parents see, Signing in, The parents' digest, Notifications to staff), `docs/spec/02-roles-and-access.md` (roles table, Student and Parent rows; Rules, sign-in; build order, first and Release 2 rows), `docs/spec/04-people.md` (Close relatives, Parent account), `docs/spec/01-overview.md` (Release 2 row; the paragraph on accounts), `docs/spec/13-technology.md` (Messages to parents; costs paragraph), `docs/spec/16-open-questions.md` (questions 15 and 31), `docs/spec/12-ux-standards.md` (Language), `docs/spec/07-journal.md` (Exemptions, the last row of Effect on totals), `docs/spec/06-timetable.md` (Everyone is told; Views and outputs), `docs/spec/edge-cases/close-relatives.md` (the rows on a new owner of a phone, a shared phone, a foreign number, divorced parents, a teacher who is also a parent, a young student's phone; two new rows on slips for a class and a forgotten password), `docs/spec/edge-cases/people-roles-accounts.md` (the two-step row), `docs/spec/edge-cases/system-behaviour.md` (services down). ## 5 November 2026: a work added by hand is named

- What changed: a classwork or "homework given" added by hand at a lesson with no plan item is named when it is added. The title is required, at most 60 characters, and is the work's column title as a plan's title would be. The teacher may rename it while the lesson can still be edited; each change is kept in history. ## 1 November 2026: the Class teacher (Sinf rahbari) of grades 0–4, grade 0, and rating exams in grades 0–4

- What changed:
  1. **Class teacher.** A new role, the 16th: Class teacher (uz Sinf rahbari, ru Классный руководитель). A class of grades 0–4 has a Class teacher in the Tutor's place and no Tutor; a class of grades 5–11 has a Tutor. Giving a Tutor to a class of grades 0–4, or a Class teacher to a class of grades 5–11, is refused. A Class teacher has every right and duty the Tutor has for own class, the Ma'naviyat soati and Homework slot lessons included. She has one own class at a time, apart from acting for another class for a period. She also works as a Teacher through the teaching load, and other teachers may teach her class too. The Head Tutor oversees the Class teachers. One definition, "Tutor of a class" in `edge-cases/00-definitions.md`, makes every existing rule about a class's Tutor cover the Class teacher of a class of grades 0–4.
  2. **Departments.** A teacher's subjects still come from one department, except subjects whose grades taught are all within 0–4: those may come from any department. A department move is no longer refused for a load row of a class or group of grades 0–4.
  3. **Grade 0.** Classes run from grade 0 to grade 11, shown as "0 London". Rollover promotes grade 0 to grade 1. A class moving from grade 4 to grade 5 keeps its room, not its Class teacher, and is listed as needing a Tutor. In Release 2, the new UID set is for the new grade 0.
  4. **Rating exams in grades 0–4.** A school setting, "Rating exams in grades 0–4", is kept by the Super Admin and is off by default. While it is off, a grade 0–4 journal of a rating subject has no rating exam column. It uses a second quarterly distribution of the subject, without the rating exam (attendance, classwork, homework, total 100), typed for each quarter. Changing the setting acts on open quarters only. It is logged with a reason, shows how many grades will change, and recalculates at once. Turning it off is refused while a grade 0–4 journal of an open quarter holds a rating exam entry, or while a group of grades 4 and 5 of a rating subject exists in an open quarter. Such a group is refused while the setting is off. A definition, "Rating journal" in `edge-cases/00-definitions.md`, makes every rule about a rating subject's journal mean a journal that has the rating exam.
- Why: the owner, 1 November 2026, in chat. Primary classes have a Sinf rahbari, not a Tyutor. She is linked to one class, teaches it several subjects, and follows the class's other subjects as a Tutor does. She holds the class's Tutor lessons, and the Head Tutor oversees her. Primary is always grades 0–4, and the school has a grade 0. Primary usually has no rating exams. The school does not want two records for one subject, and asked for a switch for the Super Admin instead. The readings the owner approved with the proposal: a role of her own; one class at a time; a department exception by grade, not by role; other teachers still teach in primary classes; the 4 → 5 rollover rule; "0 London"; one school-wide switch; a typed second distribution; the switch acting on open quarters only, with its refusals; no group of grades 4 and 5 while it is off. The proposal is `docs/proposals/spec-change-class-teacher-and-grade-0.md`.
- Code: tasks 9.4d (grade 0), 9.4e (the Class teacher) and 9.4f (rating exams in grades 0–4) of `docs/build-plan.md`, added with this change. The rollover part goes to task 9.5b (not built), as a From line. Changes to built code:

## 15 October 2026: the owner's answers on issue #42: who sees what, people and dates, the year result, no extra lessons, Tutor lessons

- What changed:
  1. **A Teacher and students (L48).** A Teacher reads the students they teach (the members of their classes and groups in the teaching load in force), with the Basic fields only, and no other student; a Teacher with no classes reads no student. "Each role sees the data related to its work." The Basic group was "all staff of the branch"; the Teacher role is now the exception.
  2. **Head Tutor and Head Teachers (L59).** The Head Tutor reads every field group of every student of the branch, Identity and Financial included (the Principal still sees Identity hidden), and exports the student list with them. Head Teachers export the student list with the Basic fields.
  3. **Tutor export (L136).** A Tutor exports the students of own class with every field the Tutor reads about them; no other class. Exports with contact or identity fields of the whole branch, or of ticked rows beyond own class, stay with the Super Admin, the Academic Vice Principal and the Head Tutor.
  4. **Diagnostic score (L50).** It has readers of its own: the readers of Financial (now with the Head Tutor), the Head Teachers and the Tutor of the student's class.
  5. **Staff member and parent (L81).** The "same person" mark between a staff record and a relative record is set by the system when a relative's main phone equals a staff member's phone, in both directions, with the name and details taken from the existing record; nobody sets or removes it by hand; correcting a phone updates it, with an audit entry. One phone number belongs to one adult (a staff member or a relative); a student's own phone may repeat a relative's. To match, a new staff record with the phone of another staff record is refused and that record shown, where it was a warning; a phone that is a relative's main phone marks the two records as the same person. The mark compares current phone numbers only: when a relative's phone equals a staff member's current phone and they are not the same person, the staff member's phone is corrected first, which removes the match (owner, 16 October 2026).
  6. **Year result (L210).** The mean of the quarter results as published (each rounded as shown), rounded once more for the year: quarters shown 50, 50, 49, 50 give 49.75, shown as 50, grade 3. It was the mean of the exact quarter results (49.475, grade 2), with "needs the school's confirmation". Rule 10 names the year result as the one figure calculated from rounded numbers; question 32 records the change.
  7. **No extra lessons.** The school never holds an extra lesson or a make-up lesson for one class. Removed: the extra lesson as a one-day change, its "counts for attendance" switch, its typed topic and hand-added works, the "replaces the lesson of [date]" make-up, the special date "extra lessons only", and "cancel and add an extra lesson" across quarters. Before, the builder refused to generate until every load had a teacher.
