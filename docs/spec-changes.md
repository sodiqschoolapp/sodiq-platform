# Spec changes

The spec in `docs/spec/` was taken from the full spec document on 3 October 2026 (revision 88). Every later change to a spec file is listed here, newest first, with the date, what changed, why, and the files touched. Changes are made only with the `/spec-change` skill and the owner's approval.

## 15 October 2026: the owner's answers on issue #42: who sees what, people and dates, the year result, no extra lessons, Tutor lessons

- What changed:
  1. **A Teacher and students (L48).** A Teacher reads the students they teach (the members of their classes and groups in the teaching load in force), with the Basic fields only, and no other student; a Teacher with no classes reads no student. "Each role sees the data related to its work." The Basic group was "all staff of the branch"; the Teacher role is now the exception.
  2. **Head Tutor and Head Teachers (L59).** The Head Tutor reads every field group of every student of the branch, Identity and Financial included (the Principal still sees Identity hidden), and exports the student list with them. Head Teachers export the student list with the Basic fields.
  3. **Tutor export (L136).** A Tutor exports the students of own class with every field the Tutor reads about them; no other class. Exports with contact or identity fields of the whole branch, or of ticked rows beyond own class, stay with the Super Admin, the Academic Vice Principal and the Head Tutor.
  4. **Diagnostic score (L50).** It has readers of its own: the readers of Financial (now with the Head Tutor), the Head Teachers and the Tutor of the student's class.
  5. **Staff member and parent (L81).** The "same person" mark between a staff record and a relative record is set by the system when a relative's main phone equals a staff member's phone, in both directions, with the name and details taken from the existing record; nobody sets or removes it by hand; correcting a phone updates it, with an audit entry. One phone number belongs to one adult (a staff member or a relative); a student's own phone may repeat a relative's. To match, a new staff record with the phone of another staff record is refused and that record shown, where it was a warning; a phone that is a relative's main phone marks the two records as the same person. The mark compares current phone numbers only: when a relative's phone equals a staff member's current phone and they are not the same person, the staff member's phone is corrected first, which removes the match (owner, 16 October 2026).
  6. **Year result (L210).** The mean of the quarter results as published (each rounded as shown), rounded once more for the year: quarters shown 50, 50, 49, 50 give 49.75, shown as 50, grade 3. It was the mean of the exact quarter results (49.475, grade 2), with "needs the school's confirmation". Rule 10 names the year result as the one figure calculated from rounded numbers; question 32 records the change.
  7. **No extra lessons.** The school never holds an extra lesson or a make-up lesson for one class. Removed: the extra lesson as a one-day change, its "counts for attendance" switch, its typed topic and hand-added works, the "replaces the lesson of [date]" make-up, the special date "extra lessons only", and "cancel and add an extra lesson" across quarters. A whole working day is still moved with a special working date that follows a weekday's timetable, and the rest of a day is cancelled with a bulk cancel. A one-day move stays inside its quarter; across quarters a lesson is only cancelled. A lesson held while its plan item is missing still takes works added by hand.
  8. **Tutor lessons.** Ma'naviyat soati and the Homework slot of a class are always held by the class's Tutor in force on the lesson's date (the acting Tutor while one is set); nobody picks their teacher by hand; a new Tutor takes the future lessons from the date of the change, and held lessons keep the person who held them. It was "the class Tutor by default". A new Tutor's appointment is never refused for a clash: the class's Tutor lessons that clash with the new Tutor's other lessons are listed in the appointment's preview and go to "needs a teacher" (owner, 16 October 2026).
  9. **Department move (L195).** A teacher's or a subject's move to another department takes effect on its start date; until then the record shows the old department, and open requests move to the new Head Teacher on that date.
  10. **Return in August (L170).** A return, or the restore of a graduate, may be dated the first day of the next (planned) academic year.
  11. **Search by phone (L157).** Ctrl+K searches relatives' phones too, for the Super Admin, the Principal and the Academic Vice Principal, and a relative found lists the relative's children.
  12. **A shorter year (L128).** Shortening the academic year is refused, with the list of what is in the way, if the days cut off hold a lesson with data, a mark, attendance, or a class or group stay; it was "a lesson with data" only.

## 12 October 2026: the staff ID format S-YYNNNN, and no support account

- What changed: (1) The staff ID is S-YYNNNN: "S", a hyphen, the last two digits of the year of the person's first admission date, and the next four-digit number of that year, taken when the record is first saved (S-260001 is the first person hired in 2026). It replaces the example SI-001 and "one school-wide counter with at least four digits". There is one counter per year for the whole school, so the ID is the same in every branch; it belongs to the person for good (a re-hire keeps it, and a later correction of the admission date does not change it) and is never reused, archived records included. Approved with the change: the order within a year is the order of first save, so staff entered later than their hire get a later number; the admission date is required to save a staff record; a 10,000th hire in one year is refused. The developer rule on identifiers now says the staff ID comes from a counter per year of first hire, taken in the same transaction as the record, with the unique constraint as the guarantee, since a plain sequence cannot start again each year. Question 12 is decided for staff and stays open for the student UID. (2) The support account is removed: the rule no longer has an account locked by default and opened by a Super Admin for at most 24 hours with flagged actions. When someone must look inside the live system, a Super Admin gives that person an ordinary account with a role, audited like any other. The rest of the rule stays: no permanent developer account in the live system, no live data copied to development or test, no names, phones or marks in error reports. In `docs/build-plan.md` task 2.2 gains the staff ID and its test, and task 2.6 loses the support account; `docs/START-HERE.md` no longer lists the staff ID format among the open decisions. No code changes: tasks 2.2 and 2.6 are not built, and no table, migration or code holds a staff ID or a support account yet.
- Why: the school wants the year of hire visible in the ID and a format that does not run out; a separate support account adds a second way into the live system that an ordinary audited account already covers. Decided by the owner on 12 October 2026, with the order of first save, the required admission date and the refusal of a 10,000th hire approved with the wording.
- Files: `docs/spec/04-people.md` (Teacher and staff record, Personal), `docs/spec/edge-cases/people-roles-accounts.md` (Staff over time; Personal data and the audit log), `docs/spec/edge-cases/developer-rules.md` (Identifiers), `docs/spec/16-open-questions.md` (question 12), `docs/spec/00-index.md` (the line on `16-open-questions.md`). `docs/spec/13-technology.md` never mentioned the support account and stays as it is. ## 11 October 2026: any number of classworks and homeworks per lesson

- What changed: the rule "A lesson has at most one classwork and one homework given" is replaced. A lesson can have any number of classworks and any number of homeworks given, including none of one kind (for example three homeworks and no classwork), in the lesson plan, in a lesson with no plan item and in the journal, with a safety limit of 10 classworks and 10 homeworks given per lesson against mistakes. Each work keeps its own title, total and column; a lesson shows one homework column for each homework given at the previous lesson. In the lesson score, the classwork part is the mean of the percents of that lesson's classworks and the homework part the mean of the percents of the homeworks checked at that lesson; the quarter averages are unchanged, each work counting once, so a lesson weighs in the quarter as many times as it has works (new worked row in the calculation edge cases). Follow-on wording: carrying a homework to the next lesson and marking a homework "not given" are per homework; when a plan item is written for a lesson that had works added by hand, its works take over those columns in order, kind by kind, with unmatched hand-added works staying unplanned and unmatched planned works getting their own column; the quality plan's rule tests add several classworks in one lesson and homeworks with no classwork. Question 36 of the open questions records the change. In `docs/build-plan.md` tasks 4.1, 7.1a and 8.1 follow the new rule. No code changes: none of these tasks is built yet, and the cells constraint (unique on lesson, student and work) already allows several works per lesson.
- Why: teachers give several graded works in one lesson, or homework without a classwork, and the one-of-each rule could not record them. Decided by the owner on 11 October 2026, with the limit of 10 of each added at approval.

## 5 October 2026: versions by quarter, and availability that changes mid-year

- What changed: a draft gets its intended start date at builder step 1, with the first day of each coming quarter as a shortcut, and the date is confirmed or changed at publish; a changed date rechecks the draft. Version history lists versions by start date and names the one in force on the first day of each quarter. The Model now says that the school publishes one version per quarter, often all four before the year starts; a version still has no end date. Publishing before a later published version offers a third answer beside "run until that version starts" and "replace the versions that follow": carry the change into them, when only teachers or rooms of entries changed. A draft that started from the draft just published is not marked out of date by that publish. Availability is a list of entries, each from a date, per branch: a draft is checked against the entry in force on its intended start date, and dates under a later entry are warnings at publish, not a block; lessons outside the entry in force on their date stay flagged until a version covers them. The row "The pattern is valid but one date is not" now lists its two exceptions together.
- Why: the school plans a timetable per quarter, sometimes all four at once, and a teacher's hours can change from a date in the middle of a version. The chain of versions already allowed this; the shortcuts, the view by quarter and a clear rule for later availability entries make it reliable and let versions be published in any order. Decided by the owner on 5 October 2026, with amendments after the check against the builder steps, the chain of versions and the out-of-date rule for drafts.

## 5 October 2026: exams in the timetable

- What changed: the timetable gains the exam: one date, a title, a subject if any, one or more consecutive lesson slots, the classes that sit it, and rooms, each with its classes and invigilators. An exam is not a lesson: it runs from the start of its first slot to the end of its last, has no journal column and no plan item, and counts for no load warning or report, but over its time it is checked as a lesson is for its invigilators, rooms and classes: one place at a time and rooms are blocked, while availability and subject of an invigilator are always warnings with a tick. It cancels the lessons of its classes in its slots with the reason "exam" and restores only those when it is cancelled; a block shared with classes that do not sit is cancelled whole or kept with the sitting class exempted. It is stored on its own and kept after a publish, acts like a lesson under a room closure, an absence and a day off, and can be linked to the rating exam of the journals whose students all sit it. In the database, exams have their own tables, and the teacher, room and class constraints run over one table of time claims that holds lessons and exams alike. The Free now board counts an exam as busy.
- Why: the school holds exams in the middle of the week in place of regular lessons. Before, the spec could only cancel the lessons; the exam itself, its rooms and its invigilators would have lived on paper, unchecked for clashes. Decided by the owner on 5 October 2026, with amendments after the check against the one-slot rule, the lesson tables, double lessons, shared blocks, the rating exam and the calendar.

## 5 October 2026: which day template wins

- What changed: a template can now also be set for a weekday and a range of grades together, and the spec says how the levels combine. For a class on a date the first that applies wins: a special date for its grade range, a special date for the branch, weekday with grade range, weekday, grade range, the default. Grade ranges set at one level may not overlap. A template set at any level takes effect from a chosen date, tomorrow at the earliest, with history, under the rule for a change mid-year. School setup shows the resulting template for every weekday and grade in one table, which is also the preview of a change; builder step 1 confirms that table. Because templates can now differ by weekday and grade, the rule for a group of two grades became a rule per slot (see the entry below). The row "The pattern is valid but one date is not" now names its one exception: a lesson that the day's template cannot hold is created as cancelled with the reason "not in the day's template" and listed, and does not stop the save.
- Why: the spec allowed a template for a weekday and a template for a range of grades but not how they combine. ## 5 October 2026: a group across two grades

- What changed: a group across classes may take students of two grades (physical education for 9 Edinburgh and 10 Brown together), not only of several classes of one grade. In lesson plans both grades count wherever a rule names a grade. At rollover a two-grade group moves up as a pair, and one whose upper grade graduates is not copied and is listed.

## 5 October 2026: vacancies, a load row for a teacher not yet hired

- What changed: a vacancy is a staff record that stands for a teacher not yet hired: a name, one branch, the Academic division and one department, subjects with grades, languages, availability and contract load; no staff ID, no account, no roles, nothing personal. A teaching load row, and a group, may name a vacancy in place of a teacher, and step 2 of the builder is ready when every load has a teacher or a vacancy.
